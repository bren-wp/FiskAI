Implemented: optional separate gross retail and base price, explicit reference date/evidence, special sale flag, retained previous price values and logical product archive; KPD six-digit format normalized to XX.XX.XX, carried into draft and DEMO snapshot. No automatic derivation of anchor price from current net price. No verified DZS catalog, no actual eInvoice mapping, no automated legal applicability or publication. Date 2026-09-10 is not a universal default; historical and new-product exceptions must be determined using the actual applicable rule and evidence.

NN 132/2026-1584 effective 17 Nov 2026: separate goods/services file structures in prescribed field order, UTF-8 semicolon CSV or XML, public machine-readable access, publication of changed lists by 11:00 on change day, 15-day archive access, per-site or shared-price rules, file naming per article 6. These publication workflows, goods metadata and audit-grade price history remain outstanding. Local records are not a compliant public price list.
